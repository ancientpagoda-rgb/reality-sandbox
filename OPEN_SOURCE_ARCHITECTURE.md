# Open-source architecture influences

Reality Sandbox / Nysa uses its own implementation and procedural simulation. The projects below are architectural references: their ideas informed the design, but their source code is not vendored or copied into the surface renderer.

## CesiumJS

Reference: https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium

Useful ideas incorporated:

- screen-space-error as a level-of-detail signal
- bounded terrain caching as a performance/quality tradeoff
- coarse/ancestor coverage before fine detail is available
- view- and horizon-aware tile selection/culling
- Sun-driven globe lighting

Nysa implementation:

- `core/surface-terrain-water-sphere-gpu-v37.js`
- `core/surface-oss-consolidation-v40.js`
- `core/surface-solar-lighting-v36.js`

## deck.gl

Reference: https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl

Useful ideas incorporated:

- best-available tile refinement to avoid flashing/holes
- bounded cache/reuse for recently visited terrain
- view-priority loading
- interaction-aware deferred work
- keep expensive data generation out of the camera hot path

Nysa implementation:

- `core/surface-terrain-water-sphere-gpu-v37.js`
- `core/surface-idle-scheduler-v34.js`
- `core/surface-oss-consolidation-v40.js`

## Earth / Nullschool

Reference: https://github.com/cambecc/earth

Useful ideas incorporated:

- cached gridded atmospheric fields
- bilinear interpolation between field samples
- spherical/latitude correction for vector-field advection
- particle presentation driven from cached fields rather than repeated source sampling

Nysa implementation:

- `core/surface-weather-v39.js`

Unlike Nullschool, Nysa's fields are generated by its own procedural water/climate model rather than downloaded Earth forecast data.

## Performance rule

The core rule discovered during Nysa surface optimization remains unchanged:

> Camera movement must not trigger procedural world generation.

Terrain, water, vegetation and weather sample the procedural simulation only during bounded idle construction/cache work. Camera rotation and ordinary rendering consume cached geometry/fields and GPU draw calls.

## Current consolidated surface stack

1. spherical topology and globe-relative camera
2. cached near terrain and opaque water
3. view-priority / best-available distant terrain
4. bounded near-chunk reuse
5. high-altitude coarse horizon coverage
6. Sun/Moon and day/night coupling
7. instanced biome/hydrology-driven vegetation
8. cached bilinear spherical weather fields
9. water/vegetation handoff stabilization
10. screen-space-error diagnostics
11. spherical horizon and rear-view culling
12. terrain edge skirts for LOD seam continuity
13. altitude-aware foliage LOD
14. Sun-coupled atmosphere shell

This is the baseline before dedicated river-channel geometry and spatial-indexed creature simulation are restored.
